# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language around getting money back after a purchase, and how that shapes what instructors earn.

## Language

### People

**Admin**:
The person who runs Cadence and decides Refund Requests. Not to be confused with the admin of a team licence.
_Avoid_: The team, Support, Staff

### Refunds

**Suspended Access**:
A learner's access to a course they bought, paused while their Refund Request is open.
_Avoid_: Locked, Frozen, Paused

**Refund Window**:
The fourteen calendar days after a purchase during which a learner may raise a Refund Request. The instructor's share of every purchase waits until it closes, even when that purchase can't be refunded.
_Avoid_: Grace period, Cooling-off period

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a purchase. An Admin approves or declines it. One purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request no Admin has decided yet. While it is open, the learner's access to the course is Suspended.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has accepted. Approving it leads to a Refund and removes the learner from the course, erasing everything they did in it. No Admin can reverse it.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has turned down, with a reason the learner is told. It has no Refund, the learner's access comes back, and they may raise a new Refund Request. No Admin can reverse it.

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund. A learner gets at most one Refund per course, even if they buy it again.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Refunded Learner**:
A learner who got a Refund for a course. Their instructor sees when they bought it and when they backed out, but not their reason.
_Avoid_: Dropout, Churned student

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund an Admin has approved where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the learner.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed.

**Refund Fee**:
Two percent of what the learner paid, which the instructor owes Cadence for every Refund. The learner still gets back the full amount.
_Avoid_: Handling fee, Processing fee, Penalty

### Instructor earnings

**Held Earnings**:
The instructor's share of purchases whose Refund Window hasn't closed yet, or that still have an Open Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Pending (that's a Pending Refund), Unsettled

**Earned Revenue**:
The instructor's share of a purchase once its Refund Window has closed with no Open Refund Request. A purchase that ends in a Refund never becomes Earned Revenue.
_Avoid_: Settled, Cleared
