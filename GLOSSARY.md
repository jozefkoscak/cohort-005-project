# Cadence

An online course platform where learners buy courses and instructors publish them.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with a reason, to get their money back for a purchase; the team reviews it by hand and approves or declines it.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, cancel my purchase, reversal request, chargeback

**Refund**:
The money given back to a learner once their Refund Request is approved; with it the learner loses access to the course.
_Avoid_: Reversal, chargeback, cancellation

**Refund Window**:
The fourteen calendar days after a purchase, the last day included in full, during which a learner may raise a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation period

**Chargeback**:
A payment dispute raised by the learner's bank or card issuer, outside Cadence; never a Refund Request.
