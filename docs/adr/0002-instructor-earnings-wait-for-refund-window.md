# Instructor earnings wait for the Refund Window, and the instructor carries the Refund Fee

An instructor's share of a purchase becomes Earned Revenue only when the purchase's Refund Window has closed with no Open Refund Request, not on the day of the purchase. This applies to every purchase, including re-purchases that can no longer be refunded, so there is one rule instead of exceptions. When a Refund is approved, Cadence gives up its platform fee on that purchase and the instructor owes a Refund Fee of 2% of what the learner paid. The service was delivered, and an instructor whose course draws many refunds carries that cost, not Cadence.

## Considered Options

- Credit at purchase and subtract the Refund on the day it happens: rejected, instructors would see earnings that later turn out not to be theirs.
- Remove a refunded purchase retroactively from past periods: rejected, figures an instructor already saw would change without explanation.

## Consequences

- Analytics shows Held Earnings next to Earned Revenue, and a period's Net earnings can be negative when Refund Fees outweigh what was earned.
- Panels that describe buyers (top buyers, revenue by country, per-student revenue) still count purchases by purchase date, minus refunded ones; only payout figures follow this rule.
