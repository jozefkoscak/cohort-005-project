# An approved Refund erases the learner's history in the course

When an Admin approves a Refund Request, the learner is removed from the course and everything they did in it is erased: enrollment, lesson progress, quiz attempts, video watch history and their rating. Their comments are soft-deleted so other people's replies stay readable. The purchase, the Refund Request and the Refund are kept, because they record money, not learning. We chose a clean break over keeping history for a possible re-purchase: a refunded learner should not walk away with their progress.

## Consequences

- Refund eligibility can no longer rely on watch history after a refund, because a re-purchase would start from zero hours. A learner therefore gets at most one Refund per course, even if they buy it again.
- Approval cannot be undone: the erased data is gone, so no Admin can reverse a decision.
